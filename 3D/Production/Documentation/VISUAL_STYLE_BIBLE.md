# Lax Attack — Visual Style Bible v2 (reference-aligned)

Status: **binding baseline for production** (2026-09-23, Blender 5.2.2 LTS, EEVEE).
Target: Mickey's reference key art (two portrait gameplay mock-ups, 2026-09-23): collectible vinyl-toy lacrosse figures on a
sunny lakeside diorama field, shot from an elevated camera behind the shooter with a miniature lens. v1 (commit `f52f21e`)
is superseded; its rejected experiments still apply.

Source: `3D/Production/StyleValidation/LaxAttackStyleValidation.blend` (rebuild with `3D/Production/Tools/style_validation.py`).
Renders: `3D/Production/Previews/StyleValidation/`. Turf textures: `3D/Production/Arena/Textures/`.

## 1. Proportions — vinyl-toy figure line
Figures are designed in a 1.5 m design space and transformed at build time (`lax_figure.set_proportions`):
body segments scale uniformly about the ground by `body_scale`, everything driven by head bones scales ×`head_k` about the
neck top. Sticks are never scaled, so they read oversized.

| Measure | Girl field #10 | Boy goalie #3 | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| body_scale / head_k | 0.82 / 1.15 | 0.84 / 1.15 | |
| Overall height (helmet top) | ≈1.46 m | ≈1.48 m | ≈1.45–1.5 m game scale |
| Head + helmet / height | ≈45% | ≈44% | 42–48% |
| Shoulders / pelvis height | 0.72 / 0.41 | 0.74 / 0.42 | Short torso, short legs |
| Glove (design size) | 0.165×0.145×0.175 | 0.20×0.18×0.21 | Gloves ≈ half head width |
| Shoe scale | ×1.28 | ×1.28 | Chunky shoes |
| Triangles (incl. stick) | ≈35k | ≈36k | Hero budget 25–45k |

Rejected: `R1_rejected_realistic_head_ratio.png` (v1) — small heads read as generic mobile athletes.

## 2. Characters
- **Kit by game:** women's field players wear goggles, a headband, a jersey and a kilt, with bare or thin-gloved hands (reference photo, 2026-09-23). Men's field players and all goalies wear the helmet described next.
- Men's field players and goalies wear a **glossy molded helmet** with a broad centre stripe, thin side pinstripes, ear
  pads, visor lip and a compact light cage (bars at brow, below the mouth and chin; verticals outside the eyes).
- Girls show hair below the helmet rim at the back, side puffs and a ponytail with a team-colour scrunchie.
- **Toy eyes:** one glossy dark oval per eye (`eye_dark`, roughness 0.12, coat 0.3) with two painted highlights, no white
  sclera; thin brows; small mouth decal; blush discs. Faces stay bone-driven (lids, jaw, mouth corners, brows, eye aim).
- Rigid toy segments meeting at hidden joint balls; no stretchy skin.
- Teams: **home** red/cream (cream jersey, red number, red shorts with cream trim, red helmet with cream stripe, brown
  gloves, white socks/shoes); **away** navy/teal (navy jersey, white number, navy shorts, navy helmet with teal stripe,
  black gloves). Goalie's chest protector is worn under the jersey colour.

## 3. Shape language and bevels
Superellipsoids (exponent 0.4–0.7), lofts and swept tubes; no knife edges. Broadleaf canopies are lumpy clustered blobs
(two-frequency surface ripple) in three greens; rocks are bevelled hulls; fences are chunky post-and-rail.
Visible cords ≥4.5 mm (pocket), ≥12 mm (goal net).

## 5. Palette (linear RGB)
Home red `(0.62,0.05,0.04)`, cream `(0.86,0.80,0.68)`, helmet red `(0.66,0.05,0.035)`; away navy `(0.012,0.035,0.085)`,
teal `(0,0.33,0.42)`; goal orange `(0.88,0.16,0.025)`; ball warm white `(0.88,0.86,0.80)`; leaves
`(0.10,0.34,0.05)/(0.20,0.48,0.07)/(0.05,0.22,0.04)`; turf base `(0.13,0.40,0.07)`, mow `(0.10,0.32,0.055)`; skin set
light/tan/brown/deep. The ball changed from yellow to white to match the key art.

## 6. Lighting — golden hour
Warm sun `(1.0,0.84,0.62)`, 6.6 W/m², angle 8°, travelling along (−0.42, 0.62, −0.66) in Blender space (from behind-left of
the goal; shadows fall toward the camera/right). Cool rim area light behind the goal. Fill = warm sky gradient only
(horizon `(1.0,0.86,0.66)` → `(0.52,0.68,0.93)` → zenith `(0.22,0.46,0.95)`, strength 0.8). AgX Punchy, exposure +0.35.
RealityKit: warm directional light with soft shadows + warm sky IBL, stronger contrast than a neutral studio setup.

## 7. Camera — recommendation to Xcode agent
- Reference-matched gameplay camera (`cam_reference`): **game coords from (−0.3, 4.3, 7.0) looking at (−0.15, 0.4, −3.5),
  vertical FOV 50°** (≈20° down). Shooter torso lands ≈22% from the bottom, goal spans ≈53–70%, lake band just above the
  fence, sky only at the top edge — matching the key art. See `03b_gameplay_portrait_reference_camera.png`.
- Current runtime camera (0, 2.9, 6.1) → (0, 0.95, −3.85), vFOV 52° (≈11° down) still works but shows mostly trees/sky above
  the goal (`03_gameplay_portrait_runtime_camera.png`). Changing it is a Swift decision; the art is built to read in both.
- Portrait frames are narrow horizontally (≈25°): framing props must sit near the view axis.

## 8. Depth of field
Stronger than v1, but the shooter→goal band must stay sharp: f/0.6 equivalent, focus ≈8.9 m (game point (−0.3, 0.9, −1.2)),
from the reference camera. Foreground hedges/props and distant canopies go soft. Accepted: `07_portrait_reference_dof.png`
vs `08_portrait_reference_no_dof.png`. Rejected: `R3_rejected_heavy_dof.png` (f/0.2) — goalie and goal blur.
DOF is optional runtime polish; gameplay must read without it.

## 9. Diorama layout and scale (Blender space; game = (−x, z, y))
Goal mouth 2.0×2.0 m at y −5.7, pipe r 0.045, net depth 2.1 m, crease r 2.2 m. Field slab x ±7, y −13…6 with a 0.45 m soil
skirt and a post-and-rail fence at the boundary. Behind the goal: hedge row ≤1.25 m, signs ("PINEBROOK FIELD",
"GOOD PLAYERS / BRIGHTER DAYS"), small bleacher with fans off the right corner, broadleaf trees at the sides only, lake from
y ≈ −19 with sailboats, forested far shore at y −46, faint far mountains, clouds. Foreground: soft corner hedges, a post with
a water bottle, rock, bag, practice balls — bottom corners only, clear of the swipe zone centre.
Ball: pockets sized for a 0.08 m visual ball radius; runtime renders 0.12 m today (recommend 0.08 visual, collider may stay).

## 10. Detail budget
Hero figures 25–45k tris; spectators reuse the builder at lower detail; no strand hair, no dense foliage cards near camera;
≤1× 2048 texture set per hero (currently none needed); shared turf textures for the arena; check at 390 px width
(`09_iphone_scale_contact_sheet.png`).

## 13. Known gaps vs. the key art (next passes)
Grass needs a normal map and larger-scale lawn variation; canopies need leaf-clump detail; lake needs a proper rocky
shoreline and lighter reflective water; helmets read slightly large from directly behind; the key art shows 5v5 field
players — the current game is 1 shooter vs 1 goalie, so extra players would be a gameplay/Swift decision.
